Real power P
Does useful work (watts)
Reactive power Q
Stored and returned each cycle (vars)
Apparent power S
1.00
Total the wires carry, V × I (VA)
Power factor
Share doing work, P ÷ S
Voltage Current Power (v × i) Shaded below zero: power flowing back to the source
Phase angle
How far current is shifted from voltage. Lag = inductive (motors), lead = capacitive.
Capacitor
Stores energy in an electric field. Current leads voltage; supplies reactive power.
Inductor
Stores energy in a magnetic field. Current lags voltage; consumes reactive power.
